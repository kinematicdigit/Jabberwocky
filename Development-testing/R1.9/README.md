# Jabberwocky Revision 1.9

This is the latest revision to Jabberwocky. It has about 90% of new design updates from Jabberwocky Mark 2 beta and maintains most of the original kit. 

The two exceptions are the new Conch mount is now flipped around and requires two M3x16 screws to secure the HEC Fan to it. The second exception is the need for a FHCS m3x8 screw for the new Draw Latch that replaces the old latch. The older latch is still available if you do not have a spare screw. 

Build instructions to follow with most things pretty much the same with the exception that the Motor mount only needs on Heatset. The Latch pin is a passthrough now and the heatset removed is now relocated to the Toolhead standoff. 

Refer to the CAD to get a better understanding of the assembly.

**JABBERWOCKY** has been engineered to meet the demanding requirements of filament-changing units (FCUs) and multicolour changers. Its extruder is purpose-built to handle the repeated load and unloading cycles of FCUs, utilizing a compound drive gear in an offset configuration, paired with dual LDO non-threaded Orbiter gears for precise and reliable filament control.

The system offers a carefully optimized balance of torque and speed, along with increased surface contact on the filament to ensure stability and consistent feeding throughout operation.

Designed for maximum reliability and serviceability, the toolhead features a tool-free hinged extruder mechanism, enabling fast, hassle-free maintenance.

Efficient thermal performance is delivered by high-static-pressure fans, ensuring airflow reaches critical areas, while the Phaetus Conch high-flow hotend enables smooth, consistent extrusion at volumes of up to 30 mm³/s.
